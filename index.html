One system from first estimate to final handover.

Most projects run on five tools that never agree. Lintel keeps drawings, budgets, schedules and site records in a single model, so the office and the site read the same numbers.

Lintel PlanScheduling and resources

Build the programme once and keep it honest. Crews, equipment and deliveries are tied to the schedule, so a delay shows its knock-on effects before it reaches site.

  • Critical path and look-ahead schedules
  • Crew, plant and material loading
  • Import from Primavera P6 and MS Project

Lintel CostEstimating and cost control

Estimates become budgets, and budgets track against actual spend as invoices arrive. Variations are priced and approved in the same place they are raised.

  • Quantity take-off from drawings and models
  • Multi-currency budgets with locked rate dates
  • Change orders, retention and payment certificates

Lintel SiteField management

Daily reports, inspections, snags and RFIs captured on a phone, with or without signal, and attached to the exact drawing location.

  • Offline-first mobile app
  • Inspections and quality checklists
  • Photo records pinned to drawing coordinates

Lintel HandoverDocuments and closeout

Every approved drawing, certificate and warranty is filed as the work happens, so closeout is an export rather than a three-month hunt.

  • As-built and O&M manuals compiled automatically
  • Full audit trail on every document revision
  • Structured data for facilities systems
